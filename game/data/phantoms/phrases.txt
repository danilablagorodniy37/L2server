#   {weapon} {armor}  weapon / armor set of the bot's grade, {grade} that grade
#   {price}           price for the weapon/armor of the line (reference price +-30%)
#   {raid}            a raid boss that is alive now, {raid.dead} one that is dead
#   {epic}            Antharas, Valakas, Baium, Queen Ant, Core, Orfen and its state
#   {castle}          a castle, {castle.owner} its owner clan or "nobody"

#   {online}          real players online
#   {need}            a party role (healer, tank, spoiler...)
